I trained as an architect at **IIT Kharagpur** (B.Arch Hons.) and moved into machine learning through research at **Universiti Malaya**, **OCAD University** (Mitacs Globalink) and **NUS**. I now build floor-plan automation and document-intelligence tools in Python as a product engineer at **SLnko Energy**.

What I like doing: taking a messy spatial or regulatory problem from architecture and planning, and turning it into something a program can check, score or generate.

## Featured

### [Rooftop solar potential, Washington DC](https://dhruv-kapri.github.io/Rooftop-Solar-Potential/) &nbsp;·&nbsp; [code](https://github.com/Dhruv-Kapri/Rooftop-Solar-Potential)

A city-scale Python pipeline that scores every rooftop in DC for solar: LiDAR surface model, GRASS `r.sun` radiation, RANSAC roof planes and PVWatts yield, aggregated to census tracts with an energy-burden equity lens, and published as a MapLibre + PMTiles web map.

| **100,064** rooftops scored | **~1%** off Esri's Glover Park benchmark | **165** tests |
